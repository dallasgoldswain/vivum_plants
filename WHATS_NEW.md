# What's new

## Coming soon

The first version of VIVUM Plants, for our pilot collectors and sellers.

- Create your account with your email address and confirm it from the link we send you.
- Sign in from your phone or computer. Forgotten your password? Reset it by email.
- Protect your account with two-step sign-in using an authenticator app, with backup codes
  in case you lose your phone. Seller owners have it switched on by default.
- If you sell plants, you can belong to more than one seller business and switch between
  them.
- A fresh look, in the colours and type of the VIVUM family.
