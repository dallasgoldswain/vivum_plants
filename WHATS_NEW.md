# What's new

## Coming soon

The first version of VIVUM Plants, for our pilot collectors and sellers.

- Create your account with your email address and confirm it from the link we send you.
- Sign in from your phone or computer. Forgotten your password? Reset it by email.
- Protect your account with two-step sign-in using an authenticator app, with backup codes
  in case you lose your phone. Seller owners have it switched on by default.
- If you sell plants, you can belong to more than one seller business and switch between
  them.
- A fresh look, in the colours and type of the VIVUM family.
- Read our terms of use and privacy notice in plain language. See and change what you've
  agreed to, including news emails, AI assistance and WhatsApp reminders, under Privacy.
  Turning something off takes effect straight away.
- Sellers join by invitation during the pilot. Accept your invite to set up your business,
  then invite your team from Sell, choose what each person can do, and remove access when
  someone leaves.
- See your plan and what it includes on the Sell page. Pilot sellers use VIVUM free, and
  we'll be in touch before any paid plan applies.
- Download a copy of everything we hold about your account from Account. We'll email you a
  link that works for 7 days.
- Delete your account yourself from Account. It's switched off straight away and deleted
  after 30 days; sign in before then if you change your mind.
- Ask us what we hold about you, correct something, or object to how we use your data,
  from Privacy. We reply within 30 days and you can follow progress there.
- Keep your plants in Collection: add a plant with just a name and where it grows, then
  fill in its botanical name, cultivar, tags and where you got it whenever you like. Each
  plant gets its own number, such as P-0042.
- Find any plant by name, cultivar, nickname, tag or number, and filter by where it grows,
  status or tissue culture origin.
- Note what you see on a plant's timeline, with photos. Log yesterday's observation today and
  it lands in the right place. Your collection holds 500 photos.
- Mark plants as quarantined, sold, gifted, lost or archived, with the date and a reason.
  Their history stays.
- Add the places your plants grow: shelves, grow cabinets, terrariums, paludariums, tanks
  and outdoor beds. Move plants between them, one at a time or several at once, and see who
  lived where and when.
- Record each plant's pot, size and watering method. When you repot, earlier notes still
  show the setup the plant had at the time.
- Record temperature, humidity, light, pH and water hardness for an environment, or your
  own readings such as TDS. Leave out what you didn't measure; it stays unknown.
- Write down your substrate mixes in parts or percentages, from about 40 common ingredients
  or your own, including branded ones. Scale a mix to the batch you need, in litres or
  millilitres.
- Record which mix each plant is potted in. When you change a mix that plants are already
  in, it becomes a new version, and those plants keep the one they were potted in.
- Share a mix with a link that anyone can open without an account. Your private notes and
  plants stay private, and you can turn the link off at any time.
- No signal? Plants, environments and recipes you've opened stay readable, and new
  observations and readings wait on your device until you're back online.
- Find short guides under Help, search them, or use the Help link beside a page's heading.
  What's new shows a dot when there's something you haven't seen.
