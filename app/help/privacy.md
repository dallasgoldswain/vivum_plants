**Privacy** shows what you've agreed to and every change you've made. Open it
from the menu.

![The Privacy page](privacy)

## Your choices

Each choice has its own switch: news emails, AI assistance and WhatsApp
reminders. Turning something off takes effect straight away. Terms of use and
the privacy notice can't be turned off, because you need them to use VIVUM.

When we update the terms or privacy notice, we'll ask you to read and accept the
new version the next time you sign in.

## History

The history lists every choice you've made, with the date and the version you
agreed to.

## Privacy requests

Use the form at the bottom of **Privacy** to:

- ask what we hold about you
- correct something that's wrong
- object to how we use your data
- ask anything else about your personal information

We reply within 30 days and you can follow progress on the same page. To get a
copy of your data or delete your account, use **Account** instead. See
[Exporting and deleting your account](/help/export-and-delete).
