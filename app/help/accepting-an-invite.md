During the pilot, sellers join VIVUM by invitation. You might be invited to
start your own business, or to join a seller's team.

## Accepting

1. Open the link in the invite email. It only works for the email address it was
   sent to.
2. Sign in, or create an account with that email address.
3. Check the details and choose **Accept invite**. If you're starting a business,
   enter its name (you can change it later).

You can also see invites waiting for you on **Home**.

## Owners

If you'll be an owner, you'll also:

- agree to the seller terms, which include how we handle your buyers' data
- set up two-step sign-in, if you haven't already. See
  [Two-step sign-in](/help/two-step-sign-in).

## If the link doesn't work

Invites last 14 days, and an invite stops working if it's resent or cancelled.
Ask whoever invited you to send a new one.
