VIVUM Plants follows tissue culture plantlets you've bought, from arrival until
they're established. It isn't for running a TC lab.

## Recording a purchase

From your collection, choose **Record a TC purchase**. Only a name is needed;
add the supplier, arrival date, how the container looked and photos if you like.

Each plantlet gets its own record and number, named after the purchase: "Monstera
TC 1", "Monstera TC 2" and so on. The purchase keeps how many arrived, and its
page shows how many remain.

If they came already deflasked, choose **Acclimatising** as the stage now.

## Stages

A plantlet's stage is one of **Sealed on arrival**, **Deflasked**,
**Acclimatising** or **Established**. Change it under **Tissue culture** on its
page, with the date it changed. Earlier stages stay on its timeline.

Any stage can follow any. A plantlet going straight into an aquarium can go from
deflasked to established without an indoor stage. Where it grows is set
separately.

## Checks and losses

Choose **Log a check** to note new leaves, new roots, damage or suspected
contamination, how you've set up humidity, a note and photos. A check is what you
saw, not a diagnosis, and it never changes the stage.

If a plantlet doesn't make it, set its status to **lost**. The purchase still shows
how many arrived, and one fewer remaining.

## The whole purchase at once

On the purchase's page, every plantlet starts ticked. Untick any you're leaving
out, then:

- **Change stage**, such as deflasking them all on the same day.
- **Log a check**, which adds one entry to each.
- **Mark as lost**.

## Suggested reminders

When you change a stage, you're offered a few reminders that suit it and where the
plantlets grow, such as checking them every 2 days after deflasking into a
terrarium, a weekly ventilation review, and **Check readiness for repotting** in 4
weeks. Tick the ones you want and change how often; nothing is added unless you
tick it.

## Once established

When a plantlet is established, you're asked about each reminder that was set up
for its TC stages: keep it, end it, or replace it with normal care such as
watering. Nothing is removed without your choice. The plantlet keeps its record,
its TC origin and its history.

## Offline

Checks and stage changes made offline are saved on your device and sent once
you're back online. Recording a purchase and acting on the whole purchase need a
connection.
