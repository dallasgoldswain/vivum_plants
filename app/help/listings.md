A listing is what you offer from one stock item: the name buyers see, the price,
photos, size, and collection and delivery details. Each gets a reference such as
L-0012. Owners and managers manage listings.

## Listing an item

Open the item under **Sell → Stock** and choose **List it**. The listing starts
with the item's name, price, size and cover photo, and your usual collection and
delivery details from **Listings → Settings**. Change anything, then choose
**List it now** or **Save as draft**.

An item has one live listing at a time. Ended listings stay in the list.

## Availability

A listing never holds stock. It shows how many of the item are available, and
**Show at most** can cap that (for example, show 5 of 20). At 0 it reads **sold
out**, and it comes back by itself when stock returns.

**Pause** and **End** never change stock. When a listed specimen is fulfilled,
its listing ends, since that plant won't come back.

## Captions

Each listing has a caption for sharing. Left blank, it follows a template: the
name, price and size, collection and delivery, and the reference. Write your own
to replace it.

Download all listings as a CSV file from the Listings page.
