## Exporting your data

1. Open **Account** and choose **Export my data**.
2. We prepare a zip file of everything we hold about your account and email you
   a download link.
3. The link works for 7 days, and only while you're signed in to the same
   account.

The zip holds one file per kind of data, such as your account details, sign-ins
and choices, in a format other services can read.

## Deleting your account

1. Open **Account** and choose **Delete my account**.
2. Enter your password, and your two-step code if you use one.
3. You're signed out everywhere and your account is switched off.

After 30 days we delete your account. We keep only a record that an account
existed, with no email address or password.

If you're the only owner of a seller business, make another member an owner
first, in **Sell**, then **Members**.

## Changing your mind

Sign in within 30 days and choose **Keep my account**. Everything is as you left
it.
