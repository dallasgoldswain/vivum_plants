**Sell → Stock** lists everything your business sells, with how many are
available.

## Items

An item is either a **specimen**, one unique plant, or a **batch** of identical
plants, such as 10 pots of the same cultivar. Each gets a code like S-0042; owners
and managers can replace it with their own. Codes are never reused.

Owners and managers set a price per plant and, privately, what it cost.
Host/packers never see prices or costs.

## Locations

Add the places you keep stock under **Locations**, such as a greenhouse or a shop
shelf. Each item shows what's at each location.

## Recording changes

Quantities only change by recording what happened, on the item's page:

- **Receive**: new stock arrives at a location (owners and managers).
- **Move**: stock goes from one location to another.
- **Mark unavailable**: plants in quarantine or damaged stay on hand but can't be
  sold. **Mark available again** when they recover.
- **Record a loss**: plants that died or were thrown away.
- **Count**: enter how many saleable plants you counted at a location. VIVUM
  records the difference as an adjustment (owners and managers).

Every change goes in the item's **History** with who made it. Nothing in the
history is ever edited; to fix a mistake, record another change.

**Available** is saleable stock minus what's held. Stock can't go below zero or
below what's held.

## From your collection

On a plant in your own collection, owners and managers can choose **Move to
stock…**. It becomes a specimen in the business you're working as, linked to the
plant; its history stays with the plant, and the plant shows as moved to stock.
While nothing is held, **Move back to my collection** on the item reverses it.
When a linked specimen is sold, the sale appears on the plant's timeline.

## Downloading

Owners and managers can download stock, or its full history, as a CSV file.
