**To ship** lists every order that's reserved and paid in full, with the oldest payment
first, so the buyers who paid first get their plants first. Orders that aren't paid in
full don't appear. Owners and managers use it; host/packers can't.

## Packing

Each order shows its plants with their codes, so two plants that look alike for
different buyers don't get mixed up. When the box is ready, choose **Mark packed**. It's
optional, and it helps you see what's left when you pack over a few days. Choose
**Unpack** to undo it until the order ships.

If a plant is missing or damaged, sort it out on the order: once an order is paid in
full its receipt fixes the plants and prices, so record a refund for what you can't
send.

## Slips

**Slip** opens a page for one order: your business name, the order number, who it's
for with their address or "Pickup", and each plant's code and quantity. It never shows
prices, so it can go in the box. **Print all slips** prints one slip per order on the
list. Use your browser's print dialog to print or save as a PDF.

## Shipping and collection

For a shipping order, add the courier and tracking reference if you have them, then
choose **Mark shipped**. For a pickup, choose **Mark collected** when the buyer has the
plants. Either way the plants leave your stock, and the order moves off the list. A
problem after that goes through a return on the order.
