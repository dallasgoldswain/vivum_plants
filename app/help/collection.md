**Collection** lists every plant you've added. Open it from the menu.

![Your collection](collection)

## Record numbers

Each plant gets a number in your collection, such as P-0042. It stays the same if
you rename the plant, and it's never given to another plant, even after a delete.

## Finding a plant

Search by name, botanical name, cultivar, common name, nickname, tag or record
number. Two plants with the same name stay separate records.

Filter by where a plant grows, by status, or to show only plants from tissue
culture. Choose **Grid** or **List** to change the layout.

## Tags

Add tags when you add or edit a plant, separated by commas. Tags you've used
before appear underneath, so you can tap to reuse them. Tap a tag on a plant to see
every plant with it.

## Status

A plant can be active, quarantined, lost, sold, gifted or archived. Change it on the
plant's page, with the date it applies from and an optional reason. Every change
stays in the plant's timeline.

Active and quarantined plants make up your active collection. To see the others,
choose **Inactive plants** or **All plants** in the status filter.

## Deleting a plant

Archiving keeps a plant's history and is usually the better choice. To delete a
plant permanently, open **Edit**, then **Delete permanently**. You'll see how many
photos and observations go with it before you confirm.
