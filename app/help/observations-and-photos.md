Each plant has a timeline of observations, photos, status changes, moves and
setup changes, newest first.

![A plant's page](plant)

## Adding an observation

On the plant's page, write a note under **Add an observation**, add photos if you
like, and choose **Save observation**.

**When** is the time it happened, so you can log yesterday's observation today
and it sorts in the right place.

## Adding photos

Add photos with an observation, or on their own under **Photos**, with a caption
and the date they were taken. JPEG, PNG and WebP photos work.

We resize large photos and remove hidden details such as the location they were
taken at before storing them.

The first photo becomes the plant's cover. Choose **Make cover** on another photo to
change it.

## How many photos you can keep

Your collection can hold 500 photos. The Photos section shows how many you've used
and warns you as you get close. When it's full, new photos are refused but your
note still saves. Deleting photos frees space.
