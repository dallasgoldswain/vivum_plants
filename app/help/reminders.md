Reminders tell you when a plant needs something. They show up on **Today**.

## Setting one up

On a plant's page, under **Reminders**, choose **Add a reminder**:

- **What:** water, feed, repot, prune, treat, inspect, or **Your own**, such as
  "check for pests".
- **How often:** once, or every so many days, weeks or months.
- **Count from:**
  - **When it's done:** the next date counts from when you last did it. Water on
    a Wednesday instead of Monday, and a 7-day reminder comes back the next
    Wednesday.
  - **Fixed dates:** it keeps to the calendar, such as every Monday or the 31st of
    each month. In a short month, it falls on the last day.
- **First date:** when the first one is due.

The form shows the next few dates as you choose.

After you log care, you can also choose **Repeat every…** to set up a reminder
for that care.

If the plant already has the same reminder, you're shown it first, and you can
replace it or keep both.

## Several plants at once

Open an environment and choose **Set reminders for plants here**. Or, from the
collection, choose **Set reminders for several**. Each plant gets its own
reminder, which you can change on its page later. Choose what happens to plants
that already have the same reminder: leave them out, add this one as well, or
replace theirs.

## Changing a reminder

- **Change date** moves just the next one. Editing the reminder later leaves a
  date you moved alone.
- **Edit** changes how often it repeats; the next date moves to match.
- **Pause** stops it until you choose **Resume**. It starts again from today, and
  nothing you missed while it was paused shows up.
- **End** stops it for good. What you did and skipped stays in the plant's
  history.

## Plants that leave your collection

When a plant is lost, sold, gifted or archived, its reminders pause. Set it back
to active and they start again from today.

## Your timezone

Reminders are due on a day, not at a time. Your day follows your timezone, which
is taken from your browser. Change it under **Account** → **Timezone**.
