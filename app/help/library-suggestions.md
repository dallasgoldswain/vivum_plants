When a plant is linked to a profile in your library and its library suggestions
switch is on, its **Guidance** section shows the profile's care notes and
suggested reminders for where the plant grows.

## Choosing to use them

Until you choose, **Today** asks whether to use library care suggestions.
**Yes, use them** turns them on for plants you add and offers to turn them on for
plants you already have. **Not now** changes nothing. You can change this any
time in **Account → Guidance settings**, or for one plant on its page.

## Suggested reminders

Each suggestion shows how often. Change the number or unit if you like, then
choose **Accept**. Nothing is added until you do. **Dismiss** hides a suggestion
for that plant until you next edit the profile.

If the plant already has a reminder of that kind, the suggestion shows what you
have now next to what it suggests. **Replace reminder** ends your old one,
keeping its history, and adds the new one.

Plants with suggestions waiting are listed on **Today** under **Suggestions to
review**.

## When you edit a profile

Reminders you've accepted never change on their own. After you edit a profile,
its linked plants say the profile was edited and show the changed suggestions to
accept or dismiss.

Turning library suggestions off for a plant hides them and keeps every reminder
you accepted.
