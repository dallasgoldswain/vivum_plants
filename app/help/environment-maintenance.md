Some care is for a whole environment, not one plant: a tank's water change, a
terrarium's misting check, a cabinet's fan. Log it once on the environment's
page, under **Maintenance**.

![An environment's page](environment)

## Logging it

Each type of environment shows the jobs that fit it first. A tank shows **Water
change**, **Top-up**, **Filter clean** and **Dosing**; a terrarium shows misting,
ventilation and glass cleaning. The rest are under **More**, with **Other** for
anything else.

Every field is optional, so you can choose **Save** straight away. You can add:

- **Water change:** how much, as a percentage or in litres.
- **Top-up:** how much water you added.
- **Dosing:** the product and the dose.
- Any job: a note, photos, and when it happened.

After a water change, **Add a reading** takes you to the tank's conditions, so
you can record the new values.

## The plants in it

The entry is linked to the plants that were in the environment when it happened.
It shows on each of their timelines, such as "Water change in 60 cm tank". To see
only these entries, choose **Environment care** above a plant's timeline.

A plant moved in later isn't linked. If you change an entry's date, it's linked
to the plants that were there on the new date.

## Changing or deleting an entry

Choose **Edit** or **Delete** in the history. It changes or goes from the plants'
timelines too. Filter the history by kind to find, say, every water change.

## Reminders

Under **Reminders** on the environment's page, set one up just like a plant's,
such as a 25% water change every week. It shows on **Today** with the other
environments, above plant care: once for the tank, not once for each plant.
Logging the job completes the reminder if it's due within 2 days.

Archiving an environment pauses its reminders; restoring it starts them again
from today.

## Offline

Maintenance you log offline is saved on your device and sent once you're back
online.
