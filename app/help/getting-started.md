## Creating your account

1. Choose **Sign up** and enter your email address and a password of at least
   12 characters.
2. Agree to the terms of use and privacy notice. News emails are optional, and
   you can change your mind later under **Privacy**.
3. We email you a link. Open it to confirm your email address. You can use VIVUM
   once your address is confirmed.

Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder, then ask for a new link from the
page you see after signing in.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email address and password, on your phone or computer. If you
use two-step sign-in, you'll be asked for a code next. See
[Two-step sign-in](/help/two-step-sign-in).

## Forgotten your password

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email
address. We'll email you a link to choose a new one. The link only works for a
short time.
