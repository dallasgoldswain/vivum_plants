VIVUM doesn't take payments for you yet. When a buyer pays you, record it on
their order so you can see what's still owed. Owners and managers do this under
**Payments** on the order.

## Recording a payment

Choose **Record a payment**, then the amount, how they paid (EFT, cash, card
machine or other), the date and, if you like, a reference. An order can take
several payments. It shows as unpaid, part paid, paid or overpaid.

Payments can't be edited. If you made a mistake, **Void** it with a reason and
record it again.

## Receipts

When an order is paid in full, VIVUM issues its receipt. Open it from the order to
see it or download a PDF to send to the buyer. Receipts are numbered in order
with no gaps, and they never change: they keep your business details, the
buyer and the plants as they were when it was issued.

After the receipt is issued, the order's plants and prices are fixed. To correct
it, record a refund.

Receipts show your **Business details**. Add them before your first receipt.

## Tax invoices

If your business has a VAT number in **Business details**, receipts are tax
invoices: they show your VAT number and the 15% VAT included in the total.
Changing your VAT number later doesn't change invoices you've already issued.

## Refunds

Choose **Record a refund** with the amount, how you paid it back, and why. You
can't refund more than was paid. A refund after the receipt is a correction:
the order still counts as paid, with the refund shown. If the order has a tax
invoice, VIVUM also issues a credit note for the refund.

## Holds on paid orders

Once an order has a payment, its plants stay held even after the hold time
passes. The order then shows **Hold time passed, review** and appears under
**Needs review** in Orders. Update the hold, mark it collected or shipped, or
refund and cancel it.

To cancel an order that has payments, refund them first.
