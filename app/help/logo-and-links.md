Owners add a logo and links in **Sell → Orders → Business profile**. You don't need a
paid plan.

## Logo

Upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 5 MB. It keeps its own shape, so a wide wordmark is
never cropped. Sale cards, receipts and slips show it. Without a logo they show your
business name.

## Links

Add Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and your WhatsApp number. Type an @handle or paste the
link. A link for the wrong site is refused. A WhatsApp number is saved in +27 format.

Untick **Show** to hide a link. Hidden or empty links never appear anywhere.

Receipts list the links you show. A sale card has room for one: choose it in **Link on
sale cards**, or choose none to show only your name.

## Changing them

A receipt keeps the logo and links it was issued with. Sale cards you've made keep
theirs too. When the logo, name or card link changes, they show as out of date, so you
can make them again.
