A recipe is a substrate mix you use, such as your chunky aroid mix. Open
**Substrates** from your collection.

![A recipe's page](recipe)

## Writing a recipe

Choose **New recipe**, give it a name, and tick the growing contexts it suits.
Add ingredients from the list, measured by volume in one of two ways:

- **Parts**, such as 2 bark : 1 perlite : 1 coir. The recipe shows each part as
  a percentage too: 50%, 25%, 25%.
- **Percent**, which must add up to 100%.

Choose **Save, ready to use** when it's done. **Save as draft** keeps a recipe
you're still working on, including percentages that don't add up yet. A draft
can't be given to a plant.

**Mixing notes** belong to the version and show on a shared link. **Private
notes** are only for you.

## Making a batch

Under **Make a batch**, type how much mix you need, in litres or millilitres.
Each ingredient is rounded to the nearest 50 mL (25 mL for less than a litre),
so it's easy to measure. Because of the rounding, the total can be a little more
or less than you asked for; it's shown under the list.

Recipes are by volume only. Weights aren't converted to volumes.

## Versions

Recipes have numbered versions: v1, v2 and so on. If no plant is on the current
version, editing changes it in place. Once a plant is potted in a version,
saving a change makes the next version, and that plant stays on the version it
was potted in. Older versions stay on the recipe page with their plants.

## Potting a plant in a recipe

On the plant's page, open **Change setup** under **Where it grows** and choose
the recipe in **Substrate**. The list offers the newest ready version of each
recipe. The recipe page then lists the plant under that version, as in it now
or since repotted.

## Sharing

Under **Share**, choose **Make a link**. Anyone with the link can see the
newest ready version and scale it, without signing in. It never shows your
private notes or plants. **New link** replaces the link, and **Turn off link**
stops it working.

## Archiving and deleting

Archiving hides a recipe from your list and from the setup form. Plants potted
in it keep it. You can delete a recipe only if no plant has used it.

## Offline

Recipe and ingredient pages you've opened before can be read offline, and
**Make a batch** works offline too.
