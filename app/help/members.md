Open **Sell**, then **Members**, to see who works at your business. Owners can
invite people, change roles and remove access.

![The Members page](members)

## Roles

- **Owner**: runs the business, including members and billing. Owners must use
  two-step sign-in. A business always has at least one owner.
- **Manager**: helps run day-to-day selling.
- **Host packer**: hosts live sales and packs orders.

## Inviting someone

1. Enter their email address, choose a role and choose **Send invite**.
2. We email them a link. You can also copy the link to send another way. It
   only works for the email address you entered.
3. Invites last 14 days. Resend or cancel them under **Waiting to accept**.

Your plan sets how many members you can have, counting pending invites.

## Changing a role or removing someone

Choose a new role and **Change**, or choose **Remove**. Removing someone takes
away their access straight away. You can't remove or change the only owner.
