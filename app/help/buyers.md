Your buyers belong to your business. No other seller can see them, even if they
sell to the same person.

## Adding a buyer

In **Sell → Buyers**, owners and managers choose **New buyer** and enter a name
and phone number. A South African number can start with 0 (082 123 4567); other
numbers need + and the country code. Each number can belong to only one buyer, so
if it's already used you'll see whose it is.

On a buyer's page you can add delivery addresses (one is the default), see what
you're holding for them, and change their details.

## Aliases

An alias is a name someone uses, such as "Sam 🌿" in a WhatsApp group, before
you know who they are. Anyone in the team can add one under **Aliases not linked
yet**.

Owners and managers link an alias to a buyer by hand. Two aliases with the same
name are never linked or merged by themselves. You can unlink an alias from the
buyer's page.

## Holding stock for a buyer

When you hold stock, choose a buyer or an alias from **For**, or type a name.

## Consents

Record what a buyer has agreed to: **WhatsApp messages** and **News and offers**.
Say how they agreed and when. News and offers is off until they agree, and a
withdrawal takes effect straight away. Buyers can also change these themselves
when they verify their number.

## Duplicates

If one person has two records, open the one to keep and use **Merge a
duplicate**. Aliases, holds, addresses and consents move across, and the other
record is removed.

## Who sees what

Host/packers see buyer names and aliases, and can add aliases. They never see
phone numbers, emails or addresses.
