An order is what one buyer is getting from your business. Owners and managers
work with orders in **Sell → Orders**.

## Starting an order

Choose **New order**, or **New order** on a buyer's page. Every order is for a
buyer. If you only know someone by an alias, link the alias to a buyer first.

Add plants from your stock. Each plant keeps the price it had when you added
it, usually its listing price, even if you change the price on the listing
later. You can type a different price when you add it.

## Reserving

A new order is a draft. A draft doesn't hold anything, so the plants stay
available to other buyers.

Choose **Reserve** and when the hold ends. Every plant on the order is held for
the buyer until then. If any plant can't be held, for example because someone
else has it, nothing is reserved and you're told which one.

Holds end by themselves when the time is up. To give the buyer longer, choose a
new time and **Update hold**. That also holds again any plant whose hold ran
out, if it's still available.

## Changing an order

Until an order is collected or shipped you can add plants, change quantities and
change prices. A changed price needs a reason, and the order shows the price you
first agreed.

On a draft you can remove a plant. On a reserved order you cancel it instead,
with a reason, and its hold is released.

## Delivery, shipping and discounts

Under **Delivery**, choose pickup or shipping. For shipping, choose one of the
buyer's addresses: the order keeps its own copy, so later changes to the
buyer's addresses don't change it. Add a shipping fee, and a discount with a
reason if you're giving one. The total is the plants plus shipping, less the
discount.

## Collected or shipped

When the buyer has their plants, choose **Mark collected** or **Mark shipped**.
The plants leave your stock. For shipping you can note the courier and tracking
number.

Payments aren't recorded in VIVUM yet, so every order shows as unpaid. To
fulfil one anyway, for example when they pay cash on pickup, tick the box to
confirm.

## Cancelling

**Cancel order** needs a reason and releases everything held. A collected or
shipped order can't be cancelled.

## Downloads

**Download CSV** gives you your orders, and **Lines CSV** gives every plant on
them.
