For plants sold at a fixed price to whoever claims first, add them to a session
under **Quick Claim** on the session page. The price starts from the plant's
price; change it if you like.

## Taking claims

While the session is live, Host View shows each claim plant with how many are
available. Choose who claimed it (a buyer, an alias, or type their group name),
the quantity, and optionally the message time so you can find it later, then
choose **Claim**.

The plants are held for that person until the session's payment deadline, then
through the backup window, the same as an auction win. On the session page you
can **Return to stock** a claim that won't be paid.

## The waitlist

When there aren't enough plants left, or someone is already waiting, a claim
joins the waitlist with a number. Nobody jumps the line.

When plants come back, for example after a claim is returned, Host View shows
**Stock back: offer to #1** with the next person's name. Nothing is held for
them until you choose it. You can remove someone from the waitlist on the
session page.

The waitlist ends when the session closes, or when you take a plant off claims.
