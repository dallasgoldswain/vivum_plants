A record can hold more than one plant, such as a pot of 5 stems or an aquatic
planting. When you separate them, split the record so each part has its own
number, history and care.

## Splitting

On the plant's page, choose **Split…**. Enter how many go into each new record;
the rest stay on this one. Splitting 5 into 2 and 3 keeps 2 here and makes one new
record of 3. The total never grows.

Each new record gets its own number and copies the plant's names, where it grows,
its environment and setup, and its tissue culture stage. It's linked to the
record it came from, and both timelines show the split.

## Reminders

The split lists this plant's reminders, all ticked. Each new record gets its own
copy of the ticked ones, which you can then change on its page.
