Record the conditions in an environment under **Conditions** on its page. Each
reading has a date and a source, and shows under **Latest** and **Readings**.

## What you can record

The form offers readings that suit the environment's type:

- Rooms, shelves, cabinets, terrariums and beds: air temperature (°C),
  humidity (%), light hours per day, and light as lux or PPFD.
- Aquariums: water temperature (°C), pH, general and carbonate hardness (°dH),
  light, and a CO2 note.
- Paludariums: both.

Fill in only what you measured. A blank field isn't saved, so it shows as unknown
rather than zero.

## Your own readings

Open **Add your own reading** to record something else, such as TDS in ppm. Give
it a name and unit; it appears on the form from then on, for every environment.

## Date and source

**When** is the time you took the reading, so you can enter yesterday's readings
today. **Source** is "Manual" unless you type the name of the meter or app you
used.

## Offline

Readings work offline like observations: they wait on your device under
**Waiting to send** and are sent once you're back online.
