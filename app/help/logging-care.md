Each plant's page has a **Log care** card near the top. It shows when the plant was
last watered, fed, repotted and treated.

![A plant's page](plant)

## Watering in one tap

Choose **Water**. It's saved straight away as watered now. The card then shows
**Add details**, for an amount or how you watered, and **Undo** if you tapped it
by mistake.

## Other care

**Feed**, **Repot**, **Prune**, **Treat** and **Inspect** open a short form. Every
field is optional, so you can choose **Save** straight away. You can add:

- **Feed:** the fertiliser, the dose (for example 2 mL/L) and the strength.
- **Treat:** the product, what it's for (a pest or a problem) and the dose.
- **Inspect:** what you found, with photos.

**When** is the time it happened. Change it to log something you did earlier.

**Other** is for anything else, such as "Flushed with RO water". Type what you
did; next time it's suggested. Fertilisers and products you've typed before are
suggested too.

## Repotting

**Repot** asks what you repotted it into: the pot, its size and the substrate.
These start as the plant's current setup, so change only what's new. Saving logs
the repot and makes that the plant's setup from that date, as one entry on the
timeline.

## Several plants at once

To water a whole shelf, open the environment and choose **Log care for plants
here**. From the collection, choose **Log care for several**; it lists the plants
your search and filters show. Tick the plants, choose what you did, and save. Each
plant gets its own entry. Repots are logged one plant at a time, because each gets
its own setup.

## Finding plants that need water

The collection shows when each plant was last watered. Filter by **Not watered
in 7+ days** (or 3, 14 or 30) to find the dry ones. Plants never watered in the
app are included.

## Changing or deleting an entry

On the timeline, choose **Edit** to correct the date, details or note, or
**Delete** to remove it. A repot keeps its date, because it also changed the
setup; change the setup under **Where it grows** instead. Moves and setup changes
can't be edited here, because they mirror the plant's history.

Choose a kind above the timeline, such as **Watering**, to see only those entries.

## Offline

Care you log offline is saved on your device and shows under **Waiting to send**.
It's sent once when you're back online, so nothing is logged twice.
