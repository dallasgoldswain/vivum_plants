An environment is where plants grow: a shelf, grow cabinet, terrarium,
paludarium, aquarium, acclimatisation container or outdoor bed. Open
**Environments** from your collection.

![An environment's page](environment)

## Adding an environment

Choose **Add an environment** and give it a name and type. Size, volume, notes
and photos are optional. Environments don't go inside each other, so a shelf in
your lounge is its own environment, such as "Lounge shelf 2".

## Moving plants

On a plant's page, open **Move** under **Where it grows**, choose the
environment and the date it moved. To move several plants at once, open the
environment and choose **Move plants here**.

A plant is in one environment at a time, or none. To take it out without moving
it somewhere else, choose **Take it out of …** in the Move list. Every move shows
on the plant's timeline, and both environments keep it in their history.

If the new environment suggests a different growing context, for example an
indoor plant moving into an aquarium, we ask whether to change it. Choose
**Keep** if the plant's context should stay as it is.

## Pot and watering setup

Open **Change setup** to record the pot or container, its size (diameter in cm or
volume in litres) and how it's watered, from a date. Repotting is a new setup:
the old one stays in the history, and earlier observations still show the setup
the plant had at the time.

## Archiving and deleting

Archive an environment you no longer use. It disappears from your environments
and from moves, but its history stays. Move its plants out first.

An environment that has never held a plant can be deleted for good, together
with its readings and photos.
