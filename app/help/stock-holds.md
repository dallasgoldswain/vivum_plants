A hold keeps stock aside for someone, so it isn't available to anyone else.

## Holding

On an item's page, owners and managers choose **Hold stock**, then say who it's
for, how many, from which location, and **Hold until**. There's no default: every
hold needs an end time.

You can only hold what's available. A specimen can have one hold at a time, and
two people can never hold the same last plant.

## Ending a hold

- **Fulfil**: the plants have gone to the person. They leave stock.
- **Release**: the person no longer wants them. They're available again.
- When the time is up, the hold ends by itself and the plants are available
  again.

Each of these appears in the item's history once.
