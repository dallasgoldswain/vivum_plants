A profile holds what you know about growing one kind of plant: care notes for
each place it grows (indoors, a terrarium, an aquarium and so on), the reminders
it usually needs, and references. Your library is yours alone.

## The platform library

**Collection → Library → Platform library** lists profiles VIVUM has reviewed.
Search by name, synonym or family, or filter by where it grows. A profile shows
its version and the date it was reviewed.

To use one, choose **Copy to my library**. The copy is yours to change, and it's
separate from the original: later changes to the platform profile never change
your copy.

## Your own profiles

Under **Your library**, choose **New profile** to write one for a plant the
platform doesn't cover. Your profiles are labelled as yours, not as reviewed.

Editing a profile never changes reminders you've already set from it. Plants
linked to it show what changed, for you to accept or dismiss.

A profile with no linked plants can be deleted.

## Linking a plant

A plant links to one profile in your library. When its botanical name matches
one of your profiles, or one of their synonyms, the plant's **Guidance** section
offers to link it. When only the platform library has a match, it offers to copy
that profile to your library and link the copy. You can also choose a profile
when you edit the plant.

Linking never changes the plant's own names or notes. **Unlink** keeps its
reminders and history.
