Two-step sign-in asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone
as well as your password. Someone who learns your password still can't get in.

Seller owners must use it. Everyone else can choose to.

## Turning it on

1. On **Home**, find **Two-step sign-in** and choose **Set up two-step sign-in**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator,
   Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password. Can't scan it? Type in the key shown
   under the code.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and choose **Turn on**.
4. Save the 10 backup codes somewhere safe, such as a password manager or on
   paper. Each one works once.

## Signing in with a code

After your password, open your authenticator app and enter the current code.
Codes change every 30 seconds.

## If you lose your phone

Enter one of your backup codes instead of the app code. Once you're in, set up
two-step sign-in again on your new phone.

## Replacing your backup codes

On **Home**, enter a current code from your app and choose **Replace backup
codes**. Your old codes stop working straight away.

## Turning it off

On **Home**, enter a current code and choose **Turn off**. Seller owners can't
turn it off.
