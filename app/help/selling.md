**Sell** is where you run your business on VIVUM. It appears in the menu once you
belong to a seller business.

![The Sell overview](sell-home)

## Working for more than one business

You can belong to several businesses. On **Home**, choose **Work as this
business** next to the one you want. Sell then shows that business.

## Your plan

The overview shows your plan and how you pay for it. Pilot sellers use VIVUM free
and won't be charged. We'll be in touch before any paid plan applies.

Owners also see the plan's limits: members, photos, AI extractions a month and
branding.
