A sale card is an image of a listing to post in groups or on Status: the photo,
the name, the price, and the size with your business name. Costs, holds and
notes never appear on it.

## Making a card

On a live listing with stock, choose the shape (**Square** for groups, **Tall**
for Status) and the file type, then **Make a card**. A long name gets smaller or
shortened so the price always shows.

## Sharing

- **Share** opens your phone's share sheet with the card and caption, where your
  browser or the app supports it.
- **Download** saves the image; **Copy caption** copies the text to paste.
- **Send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the caption, including the listing's
  reference, so you can choose a chat.

Sharing never holds stock or creates an order.

## Out-of-date cards

Each card remembers the price, name and photo it shows. If you change them, or
the listing is paused, ended or sold out, the card is marked **Out of date**.
Make a new card before sharing. Cards are kept for 90 days.
