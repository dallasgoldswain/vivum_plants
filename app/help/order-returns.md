When a buyer sends plants back after an order was collected or shipped, record
it on the order. Owners and managers choose **Record a return** on the plant.

Enter how many came back, where you put them, why, and what condition they're
in:

- **Back in stock** if you've checked them and they're fine to sell again.
- **Damaged, kept unavailable** if they need time to recover. They stay on hand
  but can't be sold until you mark them available on the stock item.
- **Lost** if they didn't survive.

A return doesn't refund the buyer. If you're giving money back, record a refund
under **Payments**.
