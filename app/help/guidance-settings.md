Guidance switches decide where a plant's care suggestions come from. Library
suggestions use built-in care profiles. They arrive soon; until then the switch is
saved but changes nothing. Every account starts with it off.

## Defaults for new plants

**Account → Guidance settings** holds the defaults that plants you add copy. When
you save new defaults, VIVUM warns you first: they apply to plants you add from
now on, and existing plants keep their current settings. Choose **Cancel** to
change nothing.

Each save is kept. The page lists your earlier defaults and how many plants use
each one.

New plants and tissue culture plantlets copy your defaults when they're added. A
record made by splitting copies the settings of the record it came from.

## One plant

A plant's page has a **Guidance** section with its own switches. Changing them
affects that plant only. The section says where its settings came from:

- **Matches your defaults**: it uses your current defaults.
- **From older defaults**: it was added before you last changed them.
- **Changed for this plant**: you changed its switches yourself.

## Applying your current defaults

On a plant that doesn't match, choose **Apply current defaults**. You see what
each switch is now and what it will be, then choose **Apply defaults**. Only the
switches change; reminders and history stay as they are.

To do this for several plants, choose **Apply guidance defaults** under your
collection. Plants that don't match are ticked; untick any to leave them.
