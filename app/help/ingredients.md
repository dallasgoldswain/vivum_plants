Ingredients are what your substrate recipes are made of. Open **Substrates**
from your collection, then **Ingredients**.

## Built-in and your own

About 40 common ingredients are built in, such as orchid bark, perlite, pumice,
coco coir, sphagnum, LECA and aquasoil. They're generic and the same for
everyone, so you can't edit them.

To change one, open it and choose **Copy to mine**. The copy is yours to rename
and edit, and the built-in stays as it was.

Choose **Add an ingredient** for anything else. Fill in **Brand or
manufacturer** for a branded product, so it stays separate from the generic
ingredient. Leave it blank for a generic one.

## What the properties mean

Water retention and aeration are low, medium or high. Particle size is a range
in millimetres. These are guidance from growers' experience, not measurements,
and the same material can behave differently from one supplier to the next.

**Intended use** and **Preparation** are notes, such as "rinse and soak before
use".

## Single materials

A substrate that's one material, such as pure sphagnum or LECA, is a recipe with
one ingredient. On the ingredient's page, choose **Make a recipe with only …**.

## Archiving and deleting

Archiving hides one of your ingredients from the recipe form. Recipes that
already use it keep it. You can delete an ingredient only if no recipe has used
it.
