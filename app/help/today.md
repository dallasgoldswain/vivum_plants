**Today** is the first page you see after signing in. It lists the reminders
that are due, whether or not you've had a notification.

![Today](today)

## What's due

**Due now** groups reminders by what to do, such as "Water · 5 plants". Each
plant shows how overdue it is. If you miss a repeating reminder, you see one
reminder for it, not one for every day you missed.

**Next 7 days** shows what's coming. You can do those early too.

## Marking it done

All plants in a group start ticked. Untick any you didn't do, then choose
**Done for ticked plants**. Each plant gets the care on its timeline, and its
next reminder is set.

For an amount, a product or a note, choose **Done with details…** instead.

You don't need Today to complete a reminder. If you tap **Water** on a plant's
page, a watering reminder that's due, overdue or due in the next 2 days is
completed as well. Undo it there, and the reminder comes back.

## Skip or snooze

- **Skip** when it isn't needed this time. Nothing is logged, and the next one is
  set.
- **Snooze** hides it until tomorrow, for 3 days, or until a day you choose. It
  keeps its due date, so it shows as overdue when it comes back.

## Offline

Today stays readable offline. **Done**, **Skip** and **Snooze** are saved on
your device and sent once you're back online.
