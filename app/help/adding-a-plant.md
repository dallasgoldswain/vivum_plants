Choose **Add a plant** in your collection. Only a name and where it grows are
needed; everything else can wait or stay blank.

## Names

- **Name** is what you call the plant. Several plants can share a name.
- **Botanical name**, **cultivar** and **common name** are optional. Leave them
  blank if you're not sure what it is yet.
- **Nickname** helps you find a particular plant in search.

## Where it grows

Choose indoor, terrarium, paludarium, aquatic or outdoor.

Tick **From tissue culture** if you bought it as a TC plantlet. That's separate
from where it grows, so a TC plant can be aquatic or in a terrarium.

## Acquisition

Record when and where you got the plant, what it cost and the order reference.
Every field is optional, so a gift needs no price. A cost keeps the currency you
paid in; we don't convert it or estimate a value.

## Changing details later

Open the plant and choose **Edit**. Renaming keeps its record number, photos and
history.
