## Reading offline

Your collection, environments and the plant and environment pages you've opened
recently are kept on your device.
Without a connection, they open as they were when you last saw them.

## Logging offline

Add an observation or an environment reading as usual. If you're offline, it's saved
on your device and shows under **Waiting to send** on that page. It sends by itself once you're back
online, and it's saved only once, however often your connection drops.

If it can't be saved, for example because your photos are full, it
stays under **Waiting to send** with the reason. You can discard it there.

## Shared devices

Signing out removes the saved pages from the device. Observations and readings
waiting to send stay, and only send when you sign in again.
