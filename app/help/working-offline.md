## Reading offline

Your collection and the plant pages you've opened recently are kept on your device.
Without a connection, they open as they were when you last saw them.

## Logging offline

Add an observation as usual. If you're offline, it's saved on your device and shows
under **Waiting to send** on the plant's page. It sends by itself once you're back
online, and it's saved only once, however often your connection drops.

If the observation can't be saved, for example because your photos are full, it
stays under **Waiting to send** with the reason. You can discard it there.

## Shared devices

Signing out removes the saved pages from the device. Observations waiting to send
stay, and only send when you sign in again.
