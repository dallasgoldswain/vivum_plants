A buyer is **Unverified** until they confirm their number. You can still hold
stock for an unverified buyer.

## Sending the link

On the buyer's page, choose **Send verification link**. WhatsApp opens with a
message to their number that includes a link. Send it.

The link works once and lasts 7 days. It shows only part of their number and
your business name, and lets them choose whether you may send them WhatsApp
messages and news and offers.

## When they confirm

The buyer shows as **Verified**. If you change their number later, they become
unverified again and any links you sent before stop working.
